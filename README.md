## Overview

ENQIVER is an early-stage initiative exploring blockchain-based financial verification infrastructure. Its research initiative, **ProofRail**, aims to make selected financial records and verification events easier to validate, trace, and review without relying exclusively on opaque, manual processes.

The project is currently focused on research, prototypes, local testing, and technical feasibility. ENQIVER is **not presented as a live production service** and has **not publicly deployed or offered a token for sale**.

## Problem

Financial workflows can suffer from fragmented records, delayed reconciliation, inconsistent data, and limited independent verifiability. These challenges may increase the time and cost of identifying discrepancies and documenting how records changed.

## Proposed Approach

ENQIVER is investigating an infrastructure layer for:

- **Verifiable records:** cryptographic evidence that selected records or events have not been altered unnoticed.
- **Consistency checks:** automated checks that can flag mismatches for further review.
- **Traceability:** clear references between source data, verification steps, and resulting evidence.
- **Integration readiness:** interfaces that could connect verification workflows to existing applications.
- **Performance research:** measurement of verification latency, throughput, resource consumption, and resilience.

These are **development objectives**, not claims of a deployed or independently audited product.

## ProofRail Research Initiative

ProofRail is the working name of ENQIVER's financial verification research track. Its intended architecture combines off-chain processing with selectively verifiable blockchain evidence where technically and economically appropriate. The design is being evaluated for security, cost, privacy, and practical usefulness.
